## Design decisions

- Chosen: three machines in one image sharing one purse: LUCKY 7, SWEET and DRAGON FORTUNE. The skeleton came from CHCraps (prefix `CHSL_`).
- Chosen: LUCKY 7 is the classic: 3 reels, one line, 15 old-school symbols and a bonus wheel.
  - Arm: hold A to pull, release to spin.
  - The bonus wheel was added at the owner's request.
- Chosen: DRAGON FORTUNE has 5x3 reels and 25 lines.
  - Features in build order: free spins, wilds, jackpot meters, hold and spin. That order was the cut order if flash ran out; all four were built.
  - Its high symbol is a lucky cat.
- Chosen: SWEET, the third machine, added at the owner's request: candy theme, 3x3 reels, 5 lines, lollipop wild, sugar-rush ladder.
- Chosen: on the modern machines A spins, and A during a spin stops the reels early.
- Chosen: no dealer anywhere ("just machines").
- Chosen: over-the-top effects (coin explosions `fx::explode`, the dragon rising in fire, ticking meters), but the style must stay cohesive with the sibling games.
- Chosen: each machine's palette swaps the three felt slots:
  - FORTUNE: maroon/jade/orange; SWEET: pink/mint/lilac.
  - The classic machine has no orange or purple (orange fruit uses SKIN, plum WINE).
- Chosen in the final polish round:
  - the machine picker is a menu of rows with mascot icons (not cabinets);
  - the paytable is on B (not hold-B), a scrolling list that auto-scrolls;
  - max bet on B was removed;
  - a welcome banner when sitting down, and big-win figures under the banner;
  - the coin shows a $ with its value under it.
- All three machines are tuned to return about 95% (the README has the figures).

- Known issue, logged but not fixed (see `../../docs/status.md`): CHSlots and CHSolitaire use the same save magic and handshake id "CHSL", and the same `CHSL_DEBUG`/`CHSL_*` macro prefix.
  - After switching games, one can read the other's save, and chdrive's `?` handshake can't tell them apart.
  - A fix gives one game a new magic and id (and updates `--id` in its `tools/chsim/chdrive.py`). That orphans existing saves of that game, so bump deliberately.
- A fourth machine is an open idea, but SWEET cost about 3.4 KB and only 3.3 KB is left before page A is lost. A machine that size would need cuts elsewhere or would give up a save page.

## Gotchas

- Device debug builds (`CHSL_LEAN`) leave out saving (stubs in `src/save/Save.cpp`), the options and stats pages, and the attract demo (`CHSL_DEMO` defaults to `!CHSL_LEAN`). `-DCHSL_FULL` forces a full device debug build.
- `tools/art/symbols.png` is the source of the reel symbols, and the owner hand-edits it (BAR, 7 and the cat so far).
  - Never delete or regenerate it from the recipes in `tools/assets.py`.
  - To add symbols, add a recipe and run `python tools/assets.py --export`: cells outside the sheet fall back to their recipe, and existing cells are kept.
  - Rows: LUCKY 7, then FORTUNE, then SWEET, each on its own machine's palette (`tools/art/palette.png`/`.gpl`).
- Reel strips are generated by `tools/strips.py` (fixed seed). After changing symbol counts or pays, regenerate them, rerun `run_tests.py` and update the README's return figures.
- `text35` (`src/gfx/Draw.cpp`) ignores `gfx_setClip`: it clips only to the screen. Clip text by hand inside clipped areas (reel windows, paytable).
- Music is the game's own sequencer in `src/audio/Audio.cpp`: one voice of two-byte notes looping under the effects (songs Title, Free, Wheel; the Options MUSIC toggle). Preview it with `python tools/audio/preview.py out/audio`.
- The play screen redraws in three bands (top, reels, bottom). `tools/chsim/diffdrive.py` (in this game's tools, not the shared ones) catches stale pixels; rerun it with `tools/scripts/diff.txt` after touching any redraw.
- Debug hooks are sent with `say`; the list is in `src/states/Screens.cpp`:
  - `R` seed, `F` force stops, `G n` force a feature (1 free games, 2 hold and spin, 3 dragon, 4 five cats, 5 LUCKY 7 wheel);
  - `J` screen (T M C F Y O S W L), `M` purse, `E` bet index, `V` reboot from flash, `H` state;
  - `Q` calibration (simulator only).
- Compiler: the simulator and tests need `CHSIM_CXX` set, or zig/clang++/g++ on PATH (see the root CLAUDE.md).
